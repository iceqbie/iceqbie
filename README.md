# (ice)^3

Graduate student in space plasma physics at the University of Tokyo
(Department of Earth and Planetary Science), working on numerical simulation
and on running it well on real hardware.

**Research interests**
- Conservation at an affordable computational cost
- Accelerating HPC simulations with AI
- Making the best use of computing resources and energy on given hardware
- How a reversible system becomes irreversible

**Currently:** writing mostly Python; learning C/C++ and HPC tools.
